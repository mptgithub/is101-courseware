NotebookLM: A Deep Dive

NotebookLM turns material you already have — readings, lecture slides, your own notes — into grounded, citation-backed ways to study: read it, listen to it, quiz yourself on it, and check every claim against the exact passage it came from. This lab walks through how it works and how to use it well before you try it yourself in the next step.

Start here What is NotebookLM?

An AI research and study tool from Google that's grounded in the sources you choose. Six quick scenes cover why that matters — click through, or have your browser read them aloud.

NotebookLM is built on retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) — meaning it retrieves from your uploaded material first, then generates its answer from that, rather than answering from the open internet like a plain chatbot. You met this idea in Topic 1; this is what it looks like in a real tool.

Voice uses your browser's built-in speech.

01 Assemble your source library

A source is any trusted material NotebookLM can read. Mix formats in one notebook to compare viewpoints, connect lectures to readings, or study an entire unit as one body of knowledge. Click a category to see what it accepts.

◆ Source-smart habit: public YouTube videos need captions — NotebookLM only imports the transcript, not the video itself. And after any generated answer, get in the habit of opening its citations and checking the original passage.

02 Nine ways to reshape your material

Studio outputs do different cognitive jobs. Choose the format that matches what you need to do next — not merely the one that looks most impressive. Select one to see details.

03 Don't generate everything — build a sequence

The strongest workflow moves from recognition to retrieval, then from retrieval to explanation. Use outputs as stages, not shortcuts around reading.

  1. 01

    Orient

    Mind Map + Audio Overview

    See the structure, then hear the central argument in plain language.

  2. 02

    Distill

    Reports + Data Table

    Turn themes and evidence into a study guide and a comparison you can scan.

  3. 03

    Retrieve

    Flashcards + Quiz

    Practice recalling facts, then test whether you can apply the ideas.

  4. 04

    Teach

    Slide Deck + Video Overview + Infographic

    Explain the topic to someone else — teaching exposes gaps in understanding.

The citation check

Generated aids can simplify or miss nuance. Verify claims against citations, compare conflicting sources, and keep your own judgment in the loop — every single time, not just when something looks off.

Quick check Before you move on

Three ungraded true/false statements — a gut-check before the hands-on walkthrough. This isn't the graded quiz.

1. NotebookLM's answers are generated only from the sources you upload, not the open internet.

2. Since NotebookLM cites its sources, you never need to double-check its answers.

3. The best strategy is to generate all nine output types for every study session.
