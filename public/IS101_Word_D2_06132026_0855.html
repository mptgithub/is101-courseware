Annual Business Report 2026
Desert Sun Consulting
Las Vegas, Nevada
Executive Summary
Desert Sun Consulting is pleased to present its Annual Business Report for fiscal year 2026. This report summarizes our financial performance, client growth, strategic initiatives, and organizational developments over the past twelve months. DSC delivered exceptional results in 2026, achieving record revenue of $4.2 million — a 23% increase over the prior year.
Financial Summary
Total revenue for fiscal year 2026 reached $4,200,000, representing a 23% increase over the prior year. Operating expenses were managed effectively at $2,725,000, resulting in a net profit of $1,475,000. These results reflect the strong demand for management consulting services in the Nevada market and our team's dedication to delivering exceptional client value.
Client Portfolio
During 2026, DSC expanded its client portfolio from 18 to 26 active engagements across the hospitality, real estate, technology, and healthcare sectors. Notable new clients include Nevada Valley Bank, Desert Medical Group, and the Las Vegas Convention Authority.
Strategic Objectives
The following five strategic objectives guided DSC operations in 2026: (1) Expand client base in the Nevada technology sector, (2) Launch sustainability consulting service line, (3) Achieve $4 million in annual revenue, (4) Hire 8 additional consultants by Q3, (5) Open second office location in Henderson, Nevada.
