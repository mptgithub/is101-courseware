The Commuter's Dilemma — Hybrid vs. EV

IS101 · Unit 1 · NotebookLM Assignment

← Back to Courseware

Objective

You will use AI and external research to determine whether a Plug-in Hybrid (PHEV) or a Battery Electric Vehicle (BEV) makes more financial and practical sense for your daily college commute. Through this exercise, you will explore a powerful AI workspace, test out different study formats, and evaluate its helpfulness as a college study tool.

The Scenario

Imagine you are buying a vehicle to get you through the rest of your college years. You must choose between a Plug-in Hybrid (uses both gas and electricity) or a Full Electric Vehicle (runs purely on battery power).

Step 1

Gather Your Sources

Before using AI, you must find 4 to 5 high-quality sources that provide hard data on car costs, local fuel prices, or charging logistics.

Examples of good sources:

  • Kelley Blue Book (kbb.com)
  • Consumer Reports
  • U.S. Department of Energy (fueleconomy.gov)
  • Local utility rate charts
  • Gas price trackers
Action: Upload these 4 to 5 sources (PDFs, downloaded articles, or pasted text) into a new notebook in Google NotebookLM.
Step 2

Explore the AI Workspace

Once your sources are loaded, click into the Notebook Guide / Studio section. Your job is to act as an independent explorer. Do not just click the first thing you see — look around the interface, test different features, and see how the AI handles your research.

Depending on what you want to try, the platform offers options like:

  • Audio or Video Overviews — listen to or watch your data transformed into multimedia
  • Study Guides, Quizzes, or Flashcards — test your comprehension of the sources
  • Briefing Documents, FAQs, or Timelines — structure the facts
  • Custom Chat Prompts — interview your data directly
Take your time exploring. Run the tools that catch your eye, read or listen to what they create, and use your own judgment to see what helps you learn best.
Step 3

Post to the Threaded Discussion

Once you have completed your exploration, post a reply to the discussion board. Write several paragraphs addressing the following:

Paragraph 1: The Formats

Identify the different options you chose to explore. Name the two specific formats that interested you the most or that you felt were the most helpful in making the vehicle comparison clear. Explain why these two formats worked better for your learning style than the other options you tried.

Paragraph 2: The Car Choice

Based on your research and your AI exploration, what is your final data-backed decision for your personal daily commute? Explain whether a Hybrid or an EV wins for your specific driving habits, financial budget, and charging logistics.

Paragraph 3: The Tool Review

Give an honest review of Google NotebookLM as a college study tool. Do you think it is helpful for your other college classes? Why or why not? Be specific about what worked well during your workflow and what felt lacking or frustrating.
