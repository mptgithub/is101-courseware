Find & Replace Text
Home tab → Editing group
▾
Why This Matters
The abbreviation "DSC" appears throughout the report. Replace all instances with the full company name.
Where to Find It
Home tab → Editing group → Replace
How to Do It
  • 1Press Ctrl+H to open Find & Replace.
  • 2In Find what type: DSC
  • 3In Replace with type: Desert Sun Consulting
  • 4Click Replace All.
  • 5Click OK on the confirmation dialog.
How You'll Know It Worked
"Word has completed its search and made X replacements." All "DSC" instances now read "Desert Sun Consulting".
